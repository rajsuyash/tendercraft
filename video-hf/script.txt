A government tender runs three hundred pages. Buried inside are the clauses that decide whether your bid is even opened.

Point TenderCraft at it. It reads every page and pulls out every requirement — turnover, certificates, experience, penalties — each one anchored to the page and clause it came from.

Then it does the part a spreadsheet cannot. It counts every obligation sentence in the document and shows you the ones no requirement covers yet. The gaps you would otherwise find after you lost.

You get a compliance matrix your team can own. Assign it, track it, export it to Excel and bring it back, without losing a single reference.

And when it drafts your response, every claim points at a document you already have. What it cannot source, it flags. It never invents.
