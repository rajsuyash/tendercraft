Frequently Asked Questions

Does AI replace the Evaluation Committee? expand_more

No. Our AI acts as a "Senior Technical Analyst" that performs all the heavy lifting—reading thousands of pages, checking compliance, and drafting scores. The final decision and score approval always remain with the human committee members.

How are scores generated? expand_more

Each member scores independently. Only once their own mark is recorded is an AI second opinion revealed, with the passage it relied on — they may keep their mark or amend it, and both are retained. Responsiveness, qualification and rank are computed in code, with no model in the path.

Is it compliant with GFR and Indian Procurement Rules? expand_more

Yes. The platform is designed specifically to mirror the processes outlined in the General Financial Rules (GFR) and the Manual for Procurement of Goods/Services. It ensures every step is documented for CAG audits.
