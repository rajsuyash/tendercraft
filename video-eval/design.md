# TenderCraft Evaluate — video design system

A deliberately different register from the bidder film. Same company, opposite side of the
table: a procurement officer must not feel they are being shown a tool built to help bidders
win. The bidder film is a light canvas with blue and saffron. This one is a dark record with
brass.

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ink | `#0e1420` | canvas. Default, not the exception. |
| ink-raised | `#171f30` | panels, cards, the raised surface of a record |
| ink-line | `#2a3548` | structural rules and dividers at video weight (3px) |
| paper | `#f4f1ea` | evidence excerpts only. A quoted document, never a background. |
| text | `#eef1f7` | primary on ink |
| text-soft | `#a7b0c4` | secondary on ink |
| brass | `#d4a537` | THE accent. Seal, stamp, gazette. One use per frame. |
| brass-deep | `#8a6a1c` | brass at rest, rules and underlines |
| seal | `#b3402f` | the sealed financial envelope, and nothing else |
| data | `#5b9bff` | figures and evaluator marks |

## Typography

- Display: **Geist** 700/800. 76-116px. Letter-spacing -0.035em.
- Body: **Geist** 400/500. 30-40px.
- Record labels: **Geist Mono** 500. 20-25px, 0.14em tracking, uppercase. Clause and page
  references are always mono: they are citations, not prose.

Numbers use `tabular-nums`.

## Personality

Institutional. The reference object is a sealed tender box and the minute book beside it.
Motion is deliberate and slightly heavy. Things settle into place; nothing springs.

## Do

- Dark canvas throughout. Light appears only as a quoted document.
- Brass for exactly one element per frame: the thing being attested.
- Structural rules. Ruled lines and a visible frame edge carry this brand.
- Mono for every citation, page number and identifier.
- Registration marks and sequence numbers. This is a record, and records are numbered.

## Do NOT

- Reuse the bidder film's blue-and-saffron on a light canvas. Different audience, opposite
  interest, and the confusion is the whole risk.
- Bounce, elastic or spring easing. Nothing in a statutory process springs.
- Use the seal red for anything except the sealed financial envelope.
- Put brass on more than one element in a frame.
- Centred floating content. Anchor to the frame edge.
